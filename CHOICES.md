# Final four cover choices

- **The Runner**: Actual running bodies and forward momentum make Snap the Whip the closest match to the poem’s physical joy, although its runners are children.

- **To a Stranger**: Sibylle’s direct, dreamlike gaze makes an unknown person feel intimately familiar, closest to Whitman’s sudden recognition.

- **The Imp of the Perverse**: Caravaggio’s tense hands and face convey divided conscience and self-betrayal, although Peter is denying guilt rather than confessing unprompted.

- **On the Knocking at the Gate in Macbeth**: An ordinary person at a doorway gives the threshold a human presence, closest to the return of everyday life after Macbeth’s suspended horror.
